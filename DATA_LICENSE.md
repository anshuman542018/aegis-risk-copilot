# Synthetic data license

`public/demo-data.json`, the deterministic generator in `lib/risk.ts` (data-generation component), and the synthetic internal policy examples in `public/policies.json` are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.

License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

All accounts, transactions, balances and loan assumptions are fabricated for this prototype. They do not describe real people, companies or financial institutions. Monitoring thresholds are illustrative internal settings and are not legal thresholds.

The Basel LCR standard is an external reference, not a redistributed dataset; its copyright is retained by the Bank for International Settlements.

The official-template PDF uses backgrounds and branding from the competition-provided presentation template. Those assets retain their original rights and are not included in the synthetic dataset's CC0 dedication or the application code's MIT license.
